About Takes.

Built for high-stakes campus discourse across India.

Why Takes Exists

Mainstream social platforms turned into passive entertainment feeds. Endless short video loops, pay-to-win verification checks, and algorithmic hostility pushed genuine conversation out of reach[span_0](start_span)[span_0](end_span)[span_1](start_span)[span_1](end_span).

Takes is built to bring back focused, text-first discourse[span_2](start_span)[span_2](end_span)[span_3](start_span)[span_3](end_span). A platform where honest opinions matter more than video production value, and visibility is determined entirely by student upvotes[span_4](start_span)[span_4](end_span).

The Core Mechanics

  • Text-First (280 Characters): Words lead[span_5](start_span)[span_5](end_span)[span_6](start_span)[span_6](end_span). Public posts can feature up to 3 carousel images to provide context or evidence, but discussion remains front and center[span_7](start_span)[span_7](end_span).
  • Public or Ghost: Choose your stance each time you post[span_8](start_span)[span_8](end_span). Publish under your verified profile to build your voice, or switch to Ghost Mode for total anonymity when raising issues regarding campus administration, hostels, or academics[span_9](start_span)[span_9](end_span).
  • Two Feeds: The For You feed offers discourse across all users, while the Campus feed is geofenced and verified strictly for your university circle.

Real Students, Verified Spaces

Anonymity without accountability breeds spam[span_10](start_span)[span_10](end_span). Takes balances free speech with safety through one-person-one-account student verification[span_11](start_span)[span_11](end_span)[span_12](start_span)[span_12](end_span). Ghost takes protect your public identity from social retaliation, while community guardrails protect campuses from targeted harassment and abuse[span_13](start_span)[span_13](end_span).

Built Independent & Free

Takes is designed from the ground up on a zero-budget community architecture. We do not sell your data, monetize your private verification details, or run covert ad-tracking networks.
