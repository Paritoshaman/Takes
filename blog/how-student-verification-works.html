How Student Verification Works

October 2026 — A privacy-first verification flow.

College Email Verification

Users sign up with their institutional email. We send a one-time code to confirm access to the college address. This is the primary, lightweight verification method for most campuses.

Student ID Verification (Optional)

For tighter campus circles we offer a student ID upload option. Verification documents are stored encrypted, reviewed manually or via an automated check, and are never publicly exposed or sold.

Privacy & Scope

Verification is used only to grant access to verified campus feeds and to limit one-person-one-account. We do not use verification data for advertising or profiling, and we commit to a zero-sale policy.
