Why Anonymous Discourse Matters on Indian Campuses

October 2026 — Separating high-stakes ideas from public identities lets truth emerge without retaliation from administrators or peers.

On-campus power dynamics often silence people who raise uncomfortable but necessary points. Ghost posting lets students report issues, critique policies, and discuss sensitive topics without fearing immediate personal consequences.

We design Ghost to withhold identifying metadata in the client, limit attachments, and keep verification data private so students retain safety while maintaining trust in the community.
