Takes: A New Social Media for Gen Z and Free Speech

October 2026 — Takes is a text-first platform for students who want genuine debate without algorithm manipulation or commercial bot farms.

Social platforms over-index on attention, short video loops, and algorithmic feeds. Takes is built to restore focused, public discourse where arguments and ideas matter more than engagement tricks.

We prioritise verification for campus communities, protect anonymous (Ghost) posting for safety, and remove algorithmic amplification. The platform is intentionally simple and student-focused.
