Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Overview

Welcome to Takes. ("we," "our," or "the app"), a campus discourse platform built for students and Gen Z across India. This policy describes how your information is stored, used, and protected while using Takes.

2. What We Collect

  • Account Information: Basic handles and credentials provided at onboarding.
  • Public Content: Public takes, upvotes, downvotes, and attached images (up to 3 images per public take).
  • Campus Verification: Student ID verification data used exclusively to grant verified campus access. This data is never publicly exposed.

3. Ghost (Anonymous) Takes

Takes features Ghost Mode for anonymous posting:

  • Ghost posts completely omit your username, avatar, and account link on the client feed.
  • Ghost posts do not allow attached photo uploads to prevent accidental metadata leakage.

4. Storage & Zero-Sale Commitment

We run a student-first architecture. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data or verification documents to data brokers or advertising networks.

5. Contact

If you have privacy concerns, email us at privacy@takes.example (placeholder) or open an issue in our repository.
