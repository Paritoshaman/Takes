What is Takes?

Takes is a text-first microblogging app where real people post short takes of up to 280 characters, publicly or anonymously.[span_11](start_span)[span_11](end_span)

Who is Takes for?

Students and Gen Z in India who want to connect with their campus and each other.

Will Takes have bots, reels or AI images?

No reels.[span_12](start_span)[span_12](end_span) Takes is text-first.[span_13](start_span)[span_13](end_span) The platform is built around verified students to make bot farming unprofitable.

Can people see who wrote an anonymous take?[span_14](start_span)[span_14](end_span)

No. Other users cannot see the author of an anonymous take. Identity is protected.
