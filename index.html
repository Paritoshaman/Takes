Questions

What is Takes?

Takes is a text-first microblogging platform where real students publish short takes up to 280 characters, either publicly or anonymously.

Who can use Takes?

Built primarily for students and Gen Z across India to unite campuses. Verified journalists, institutions, and student groups can also participate.

Can anyone see who wrote a Ghost take?

No. Public usernames and profile links are completely uncoupled from Ghost takes. Metadata is kept private to preserve discourse integrity.
