Frequently Asked Questions

What is Takes?

Takes is a text-first campus microblogging platform where verified students publish short takes up to 280 characters, publicly or via anonymous Ghost Mode.

Who can sign up for Takes?

Takes is built for college students and Gen Z in India. Anyone can browse the global For You feed, but verified Campus feeds require student verification.

Can people see who wrote a Ghost take?

No. Ghost mode completely removes your name, handle, and avatar from the client feed. Identity data is kept strictly detached to safeguard free expression.

Will Takes have reels, short video, or paid verification?

Never. Takes is strictly text and static photo carousels. Verification is earned through student credentials, not purchased with credit cards.
